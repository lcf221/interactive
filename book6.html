Beg, Borrow, or Steal by Sarah Adams is a small-town, enemies-to-lovers rom-com following Emily Walker, a perfectionist second-grade teacher, and her rival, Jack Bennett, who returns to town as her neighbor. When Emily accidentally emails a secret, passionate manuscript to her principal, she must rely on Jack—a secret bestselling author—to help her.
