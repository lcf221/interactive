In A Good Girl's Guide to Murder by Holly Jackson, 17-year-old Pippa "Pip" Fitz-Amobi investigates the 5-year-old closed murder case of Andie Bell, who was allegedly killed by her boyfriend, Sal Singh, before he committed suicide. Believing Sal was innocent, Pip uncovers dark secrets in her town, putting her own life in danger to find the real killer.
