9/11 Series

Stories inspired by 9/11, courage, hope, and faith in the face of tragedy.

One Tuesday Morning

One Tuesday Morning by Karen Kingsbury is a faith-based 9/11 drama following NYC firefighter Jake Bryan, who suffers amnesia after the Twin Towers collapse. While his wife, Jamie, helps him rebuild his life and faith, a twist involves Eric Michaels, a California businessman who was in the towers and lost his memory, creating a story of redemption, love, and loss.

★★★★★

Beyond Tuesday Morning

Beyond Tuesday Morning by Karen Kingsbury is a faith-based novel set three years after the 9/11 attacks. It follows FDNY widow Jamie Bryan as she finds healing while volunteering at St. Paul's Chapel, navigating new love, and facing the brother of the man who impersonated her deceased husband.

★★★★☆

Remember Tuesday Morning

Remember Tuesday Morning by Karen Kingsbury is a Christian fiction novel following Alex Brady, a man deeply scarred by his father’s death in the 9/11 attacks. Consumed by grief and a desire to fight evil, Alex works with his K9 partner, Bo, until a series of California wildfires forces him to confront his past and open his heart to love and faith again.

★★★★★
