Redemption by Karen Kingsbury (with Gary Smalley) is the first book in the Baxter Family series, focusing on Kari Baxter Jacobs, who fights to save her marriage after her husband, Tim, confesses to an affair with a student and asks for a divorce. Grounded in the theme "love is a decision," the story highlights Kari's commitment to faith and forgiveness despite deep heartbreak.
