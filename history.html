My favorite Historical Fiction is The Help! This book was emotional, entertaining, and a beautiful lesson. Set in 1960s Mississippi, this book follows white aspiring writer Eugenia "Skeeter" Phelan and African American maids Aibileen Clark and Minny Jackson.They risk their safety to write a book exposing the harsh realities and injustices faced by Black domestic workers. The narrative centers on themes of racism, friendship, and courage.
