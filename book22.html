The American Roommate Experiment by Elena Armas is a contemporary romance novel about Rosie Graham, a secret romance writer who, after her apartment ceiling collapses, must move in with her best friend's cousin, Lucas Martín, leading to a fake dating/experimental dates plot to help her overcome writer's block. It's a companion novel to The Spanish Love Deception, featuring the same characters, and became a New York Times bestseller, known for its "nice guy" hero and tropes like friends-to-lovers and fake dating.
