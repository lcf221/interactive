Just for the Summer by Abby Jimenez is a witty contemporary romance about Emma, a traveling nurse, and Justin, who make a pact to date and break up to cancel out a supposed "curse" where his exes find their soulmates immediately after their breakup. The plan goes awry when they develop real feelings while navigating unexpected life challenges, including Justin taking custody of his three younger siblings and Emma's difficult mother showing up. The book, published in April 2024, is known for its humor, emotional depth, and handling of mental health themes, and is part of the Part of Your World series.
